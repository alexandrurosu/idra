What these are

In IDRA, code is not the thing people read. People own the intent, and they read views of the system generated for what they need to judge: an auditor sees trust boundaries, an operator costs and failure paths, a reviewer the trace from intent to evidence (Chapters 3 and 4). These pages are those views for the proof of concept's cache, generated from its intent file, the build's evidence and the program itself.
